# Printable QR leave-behind card

Print at 85 × 55 mm (business card), matte, one side.

**Front**
- 615 Septic Tank Pumping logo, top left
- Heading: "How did we do?"
- Body: "A quick review helps your neighbours find someone who opens the tank and tells them what's in it."
- QR code linking to the review destination, centred, minimum 25 mm square
- Footer: (615) 234-9048 · 615septictankpumping.com

**Generating the QR code:** point it at the Google review URL, not at the
website. One tap should land on the review form.

**Rules:** the card offers nothing in exchange for a review. No discount, no
prize draw, no "leave us five stars". It asks for an honest review and nothing more.
